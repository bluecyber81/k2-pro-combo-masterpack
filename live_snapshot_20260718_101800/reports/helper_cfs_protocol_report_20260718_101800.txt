== CFS protocol and slot report ==
Safety: read-only only. This does not load, unload, extrude, cut, refresh or send raw BOX/RS485 commands.
Bus:
- device: /dev/ttyS5
- baud: 230400
- stack: serial_485 -> auto_addr -> box_wrapper -> filament_rack_wrapper
- frame: head 0xF7, addr, len, status, function, data, crc8(poly 0x07)
- material-box addresses: 0x01..0x04, shown as T1A..T4D

Risk policy:

- Avoid blindly: BOX_SEND_DATA, BOX_INFO_REFRESH, BOX_SET_PRE_LOADING, BOX_LOAD_MATERIAL, BOX_EXTRUDE_MATERIAL, BOX_RETRUDE_MATERIAL, raw _CFS_LOAD/_CFS_UNLOAD.
