### helper_nozzle_camera_diagnose
### command: sh /mnt/UDISK/helper-script/helper.sh --nozzle-camera-diagnose

K2 Pro nozzle AI camera diagnosis - read-only
This does not power-cycle the nozzle camera. Creality may keep it off/standby while idle and enable it only for AI, flow or first-layer checks.
If Fluidd/Mainsail main camera works but Creality Print app hides camera after firmware updates, treat that as app/firmware compatibility first.
If a USB hub is attached to the chamber-camera path, stock firmware may bind the first device as the chamber camera and leave the nozzle path wrong.
